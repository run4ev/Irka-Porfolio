P.C. A rebrand for a primary-care assistant app. The identity is built from two simple shapes, a
circle for the P and a square for the C, in a duotone blue palette: cobalt on pale blue-grey. Clean
lines keep the experience streamlined and intuitive; the colour keeps it calm and clear.
