[02] Kebeta

KEBETA branding identity is designed for a workshop atelier that doubles as a coworking space, blending creativity and collaboration. It reflects a shared spirit of craftsmanship, innovation, and community.

This identity is inspired by the concept of repurposing plywood leftovers. It embodies sustainability, creativity, and the beauty of transforming remnants into something meaningful
